# Business plan — Greenularity Caribbean™ (sanitized public)

## Executive summary

**Greenularity Caribbean™** is an energy intelligence and implementation pathway for Caribbean households and businesses. The customer journey is: **bill → understand → diagnose → optimize → (finance) → implement → verify**.

This Hult venture submission ships a **public production demonstration** (Energy Auditor) that covers the first steps — baseline analytics, illustrative savings, audit-readiness, and a CEAL Green professional-audit call-to-action. Full product vision (bill OCR, room vision, incentive navigator, six-month M&V) is phased on the roadmap and is **not** reproduced as proprietary methodology in this public repo.

Revenue follows a hybrid **SaaS + engineering services** model through CEAL Green.

## Customer problem

Electricity is a major operating cost. Customers receive bills but lack coordinated information on loads, retrofit economics, financing, and qualified implementation. See [market-research.md](./market-research.md).

## Target customers

| Segment | Examples |
|---------|----------|
| **Residential** | Higher-consumption homes (AC, water heating, pools) |
| **Commercial / SME** | Restaurants, hotels, retail, medical, offices |
| **Institutional** | Schools, clinics, public buildings |

Initial markets: Trinidad & Tobago, Jamaica, Barbados, Guyana, St Kitts & Nevis — **sequential** launch.

Production URL: **https://caribbeanenergyauditor.vercel.app**

## Market assessment

Public census dwelling counts, utility tariff pages, and IDB/CDB policy literature support demand for digital efficiency tools. Details: [market-research.md](./market-research.md).

## Business model

1. **Free** preliminary analysis (calculator / digital baseline)
2. **Subscription** (roadmap) — monitoring, alerts, detailed digital audit
3. **Professional audit & implementation** — CEAL Green engineering revenue
4. **Optional** verified savings monitoring (six-month M&V cycle)

See [marketing-investment-plan.md](./marketing-investment-plan.md) for GTM and unit-economics **targets** (preliminary).

## Go-to-market

Partner-led, savings-led acquisition — chambers, banks, property managers, community pilots. First pass gate: **≥25 Ludwitt-verified external users** completing the calculator.

## Delivery and support

- Public demo: Vercel + Ludwitt/Hult authentication
- Professional services: CEAL Green qualified personnel (off-platform)
- Privacy: no bill images stored server-side in public demo

## Development roadmap (12-month summary)

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|--------|

| Months 3–4 | Alpha: auth, bill ingestion, T&T tariff engine |
| Months 5–6 | MVP: audit workflow, M&V hooks, **25+ external users** |
| Months 7–8 | Validate economics & conversions |
| Months 9–10 | Barbados & Jamaica configuration |
| Months 11–12 | Paid plans, enterprise dashboard |

## Key risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Overstated savings | Ranges, disclaimers, engineer validation |
| Tariff drift | Date-stamped public sources |
| Trust | CEAL Green implementation + M&V |
| Metrics gaming | Ludwitt launcher + qualification rules |
