# Elevator pitch — Greenularity Caribbean™ (sanitized public)

**cEAL Green Energy Limited · public venture submission · August 2026**

---

## One-minute pitch

Caribbean households and businesses receive electricity bills every month, but most do not know which equipment is driving the cost, what changes will produce the fastest savings, or how to finance improvements.

**Greenularity Caribbean™** turns the electricity bill into an energy-reduction pathway. A customer establishes a baseline from their bill, completes a guided preliminary audit, and receives illustrative savings ranges, payback estimates, and audit-readiness guidance. Qualified users can request a **CEAL Green** professional site assessment for engineering validation and implementation.

We track subsequent bills to compare **expected versus actual** savings over time — building trust through measurement, not guarantees.

We are not building “just another calculator.” This public demonstration is the **digital front door** to Caribbean energy-efficiency services: identify waste → quantify opportunity → connect to professional audit → verify results.

---

## Problem

- High and volatile electricity costs affect household affordability and SME margins.
- Bills show *what* you paid, not *where* energy went.
- Financing, incentives, and qualified installers are fragmented across jurisdictions.

---

## Solution (MVP scope — this demo)

| Step | Customer action | Platform output |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Launch authenticated session | Secure access to calculator |
| 2 | Enter bill data (spend, kWh, loads) | Baseline consumption & spend |
| 3 | Compare jurisdictions | Illustrative tariff references (public sources) |
| 4 | Review savings scenarios | Low / medium / high **ranges** |
| 5 | Audit readiness score | Recommendation + CEAL Green CTA |

*Full product vision includes bill capture, room walkthrough, incentive navigator, and six-month M&V — phased on the public roadmap.*
