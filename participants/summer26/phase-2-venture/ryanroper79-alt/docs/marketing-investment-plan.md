# Marketing & investment plan — Greenularity Caribbean™ (sanitized public)

**Preliminary assumptions labeled throughout · August 2026 · No confidential CRM or client names**

---

## 1. Investment thesis

Greenularity Caribbean™ is positioned as a **Caribbean energy-cost reduction and implementation platform**, not merely an audit spreadsheet.

**Commercial flywheel:**

```
Acquire customer → analyze bill → diagnose use → quantify savings →
identify financing → implement → verify savings → retain customer
```

**Revenue pools (high level):**

1. Recurring SaaS (bill monitoring, alerts, digital audit)
2. CEAL Green engineering, audit, installation, and M&V services
3. Future transaction revenue (equipment, financing referrals, enterprise licensing) — subject to conflict-of-interest controls

---

## 2. Why the economics make sense

The customer pain point is tangible: **the monthly electricity bill**.

Marketing can lead with plain language:

> “How can I reduce my light bill?”

Then deliver a quantified proposition:

> Spend **X** → save **Y/month** (illustrative range) → payback **Z** months (verify professionally before capital spend).

**Regional nuance (public sources):**

- **Trinidad & Tobago:** Lower published residential tiers ([T&TEC](https://ttec.co.tt/tariffs-2/)) — strong market to prove product and trust; payback economics vary by load profile.
- **Barbados / Jamaica:** Higher visible energy + fuel-adjustment components — stronger consumer urgency ([BLPC](https://www.blpc.com.bb/residential/residential-tariffs-riders/), JPS tools).

> “Upload your electricity information and find out where your money is going.”

**Monetization ladder:**

Free bill analysis → subscription (roadmap) → CEAL Green site visit → implementation → six-month bill monitoring.

---

## 4. Marketing message architecture

**Customer promise:** *Lower your electricity bill. Prove the savings.*

| Layer | Message |
|-------|---------|
| Consumer | “Why is my light bill so high?” |
| Solution | “Find where electricity is being wasted.” |
| Economic | “See what to change, what it costs, and when it pays back.” |
| Execution | “CEAL Green can implement and track your bills.” |

For SMEs: **Lower energy cost → improve margin → fund growth.**

Avoid leading consumer ads with jargon (dMRV, Scope emissions) — use outcome language first.

## 9. Pipeline approach (sanitized)

A internal CRM target list exists for **commercial activation** (government ministries, banks, retail chains, hospitality, etc.) — **not published** in this repository to avoid disclosing named prospects pre-outreach.

Public GTM instead documents:

- **Tier A:** high-intensity or portfolio-scale prospects → executive pilot
- **Tier B:** credible SME/government → facilities lead + digital demo
- **CTA:** 30-minute energy-cost discovery → one-site baseline audit
- **Rule:** do not promise a fixed % saving before baseline; sell **identify → implement → measure**
