## Critique prompt

Copy this into an AI chat. Paste your write-up under the line. The AI should critique the plan and should not give you solution code.

```text
I am a student practicing LeetCode. Below is my planning write-up. Critique the plan only.

Do not write solution code. Do not repair my code. Do not outline a full algorithm I could type in. If I pasted a Python solution, ignore it.

Please do these things:
1. Find gaps in the plan.
2. Name edge cases I missed.
3. Check my Big-O time and space. If a bound is wrong, say why, without showing a corrected implementation.
4. Ask Socratic questions that help me find the fix myself.

Be direct and short.

---
PASTE YOUR WRITE-UP BELOW THIS LINE
```
