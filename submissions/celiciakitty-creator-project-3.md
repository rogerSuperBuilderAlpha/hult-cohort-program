# Project 3 Submission — @celiciakitty-creator

**Cohort in Bloom** presents Cohort 67 as a living digital garden where ideas take root, projects grow through rapid weekly challenges, and participant work becomes easier for partners, mentors, recruiters, and collaborators to discover. The floral visual system is soft and customisable while the information architecture remains clear, accessible, and professional.
