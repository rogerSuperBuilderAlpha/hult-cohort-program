## Partner-facing README

**What Comentiq is:** a live showcase of builders in the Hult Summer
Cohort 2026 — their projects, verified progress, and what they need
next to grow. If you're exploring pilot partnerships, mentorship,
sponsorship, or early hiring, this is where to find builders whose
work matches what you're looking for.

**How to explore:**
- Browse [Projects](https://comentiq-lorra-v.vercel.app/projects) filtered loosely by
  stage — idea, building, launched, pilot, scaling — to find builders
  at the point where your involvement would matter most.
- Browse [Builders](https://comentiq-lorra-v.vercel.app/builders) to see the people
  behind the work, their skills, and what they're building.
- Every project page shows real progress updates and AI-assisted
  campaign content — reviewed and approved by the builder, never
  auto-published — so what you're reading reflects what's actually
  true, not a marketing gloss.

**How to engage:** every project and builder page has an "Express
interest" link that opens a short enquiry form — tell us what kind
of partnership you're considering (pilot, mentorship, sponsorship,
hiring, investment, or something else) and which builder or project
caught your interest. A cohort admin reviews every enquiry and
follows up directly.

**What's still early:** this is a Phase 1 pilot build. Partner
discovery currently works through direct browsing and enquiries
rather than filtered search — we're prioritizing getting real
partner conversations started over building out every discovery
feature first.
