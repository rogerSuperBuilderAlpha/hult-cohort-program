How to use this site

A quick walkthrough of each tab, so you know what to do and what to expect.

1. Cohort wall

This is the public view everyone sees, including partners. Search by name or project, or filter to only see projects that have shipped. Cards update live as people submit — hit "Refresh" if you want the latest without reloading the page.

2. Add my project

Fill in your name, an optional handle, and your project details. You must set a passcode — write it down somewhere, since you'll need it to edit or delete your profile later, and there's no "forgot passcode" recovery. If you haven't shipped a project yet, leave the project fields blank and your card will show "coming soon" until you update it.

Below the submit form is a separate "Edit or delete your profile" box — enter your name and passcode there any time to update your info, remove yourself entirely, or add more projects. Each additional project can have a title, tagline, and up to three optional links: a live site, a GitHub repo, and a demo video. Only the title is required per project.

3. Peer review

Use this to review a peer's project. Enter your own name (reviews aren't anonymous), the exact name of the person you're reviewing, your written feedback, and an up-vote or abstain. Reviews are not posted publicly — only visible to the project owner and the organizer, via the lookup box at the bottom of the same tab.

4. For partners

This is what we send to anyone outside the cohort considering partnering, hiring, investing, or piloting a project. Worth a read so you know how your work is being framed to outsiders.

A few things worth knowing

  • Your passcode is stored securely, but there's no recovery if you forget it — you'd need to contact the organizer.
  • Submitting too quickly (several times within a minute) will be blocked temporarily as a spam protection measure — this is normal, just wait a moment and try again.
  • Anyone can report a profile that seems inappropriate; three reports auto-hides it pending organizer review.

Partner with the cohort

Hult Cohort 67 is a summer program of 66 builders shipping real, individually-built projects over weekly contest cycles. This page is for anyone considering partnering with, hiring from, investing in, or piloting a project from the cohort.

What this program is

Each week centers on a themed build. Week 3 is vibe marketing: every participant ships a production site or campaign artifact for their own project, then reviews their peers' submissions before a fixed deadline. Participants work individually, and submissions appear on the wall as they merge.

What "peer reviewed" means here

Every merged submission gets a written technical review from at least one peer, plus an optional up-vote or abstain. This is an internal quality signal between builders — not an external audit, accreditation, or endorsement. If you're evaluating a project for partnership or investment, we'd encourage your own independent diligence.

Ways to engage

  • Pilot a project — reach out directly to a builder whose work fits a real problem you have.
  • Hire — many participants are looking for their next role; profiles link to their work.
  • Invest or fund — builders are open to conversations if a project has commercial legs.
  • Mentor or advise — short-term guidance is welcome and often high-leverage this early.

How to contact a builder

Every profile on the cohort wall links to that builder's project and, where provided, a handle. If a profile doesn't list contact details, reach out to the cohort organizer for an introduction.
