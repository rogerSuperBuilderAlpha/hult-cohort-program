# Pulse — partner guide

**Audience:** Hiring partners evaluating the Hult Cohort Program Summer Pilot 2026.

## What Pulse is

Pulse is the cohort's **vibe marketing surface** — live telemetry, builder profiles, and project evidence designed to convert partner curiosity into action. It integrates read-only status from the winning PM platform (Forth) and links to cohort comms and individual deploys.

**Core pitch:** Don't trust our word — inspect their GitHub.

## How to evaluate a builder in ~10 minutes

1. Open **Builders** and scan signature projects + stack badges.
2. Open a profile → GitHub, live deploys, Quick Connect.
3. Check homepage **Cohort project status** (Forth PM snapshot).
4. Review **Showcase** cards — toggle **Under the Hood** for architecture flow.
5. Submit **Request intro** on `/partners` (or Quick Connect on any profile).

## Contact

Submissions are stored in Supabase `partner_inquiries` and logged server-side for placement lead follow-up within one business day.

## PM / comms integration

Pulse reads cohort initiative status from **Forth** (winning PM) and surfaces links to **Cohort Comms**. Individual builder deploys appear on profiles when provided.

## Privacy

Profiles are **opt-in public** by default for listed cohort members. Opt-out shows a private placeholder (not yet exercised in Summer Pilot roster).
