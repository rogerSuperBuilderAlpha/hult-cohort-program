01 · Problem

The owner's day is a repeat-question machine.

Small businesses miss 20–35% of inbound calls during business hours and ~62% after hours; missed calls cost US SMBs >$81B/yr — $40k–$100k per owner. Owners "get asked the same 10–15 questions hundreds of times": hours? do you do X? how much is Y? do you deliver?

02 · Solution

Paste a URL. Drop a script tag. Visitors get grounded answers.

1

Paste your website — we crawl, chunk, and index your pages.

2

Copy one script tag to your site — no signups for your customers, no credentials on the page.

3

Answers with the source — visitors see the exact page the answer came from. Accurate by construction.

Keyless · always works

BM25-lite retrieval + template answer = instant, zero-cost, no API key. LLM upgrades when the owner adds one.

Guardrails by default

20 calls/assistant/day cap, 400-token answers, 30-day retention — no hallucination floor, no invoice shock.
